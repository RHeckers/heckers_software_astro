## Situation

Vattenfall gives more than two million customers insight into what they use and what it costs. Usage and costs is the heart of the consumer app. The section was built in Swift and Kotlin, and its charts stuttered on mobile. I worked at Vattenfall from August 2023 to January 2025, in several teams, and was lead front-end developer for dynamic tariffs and TijdPrijs.

## What I did

- Redeveloped the whole usage and costs section with my team, and migrated it from Swift and Kotlin to Angular with Capacitor and Ionic.
- Fixed the chart performance on mobile with caching, scrolling and rendering changes: from stuttering to smooth.
- Implemented atomic design in the Angular UI component libraries.
- Built the front end, the back end and the database parts of the "Vooruit" loyalty programme.
- Set up CI/CD pipelines and improved the Nx Cloud setup.
- Coached junior developers, improved the structure of the Nx monorepo, and raised test coverage and WCAG 2.1 conformance.

## Result

NPS for the usage and costs section rose from 3.4 to 4.2. Campaigns and prize draws in the loyalty programme can be rolled out at scale. The pipelines run more in parallel, with fewer regressions and a clearer view of what fails.
