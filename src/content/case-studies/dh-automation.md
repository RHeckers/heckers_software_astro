## Situation

DH Automation had to deliver an application to its end customer on a hard deadline: software that takes orders from the Exact accounting software, divides them into batches and sends them to the machines on the factory floor. It was not production ready. I was the only software engineer on it, next to a PLC developer, from November 2018 to January 2019.

## What I did

- Improved the APIs, the MongoDB structures and the heavy data visualisation components.
- Added the missing pages, API routes and connections to Exact and the PLCs.
- Implemented login with JWT and OAuth, on the front end and the back end.
- Fixed the bugs, raised the test coverage and set up the CI/CD pipelines.
- Added filters and search for the operators.

## Result

The application was production ready before the deadline, and the client launched on time. Performance and stability went up.
